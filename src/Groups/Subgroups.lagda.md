### Subgroups

This is the `Groups.Subgroups` module of the agda-groups library.

It is a barrel module: it declares nothing of its own and re-exports the
modules under `Groups/Subgroups/`.  A subgroup of a finite group is the
Agda Universal Algebra Library's decidable subgroup, `DecSubgroup`, and
two subgroups are equal when they have the same elements, `≐` (ADR 0001,
§§ 2 and 3).  Each module holds one module parameterized by the finite
group, to be opened with `using`: `Subgroups 𝔾`, `Generated 𝔾`,
`AsGroup 𝔾` and `Notions 𝔾`.  The modules are the following:

+  `Groups.Subgroups.Basic`: the order |H| of a subgroup, inclusion and
   equality decided, and the subgroups that later modules build, each
   with its decider: the trivial and whole subgroups, intersections,
   conjugates, normalizers, the decider remembered (`memo`),
   centralizers, normal closures and the set HK of products;
+  `Groups.Subgroups.Generated`: the subgroup ⟨A⟩ generated by a list A,
   with its universal property, decided by a certified Schreier vector;
   and inclusion, normalizers and conjugates decided by the generators
   alone;
+  `Groups.Subgroups.Group`: a subgroup as a finite group of the same
   order, and the passage of subgroups between it and the group;
+  `Groups.Subgroups.Notions`: normal, abelian and elementary abelian
   subgroups, the subgroups between two, complements, and minimal normal
   subgroups, with the theorem that every nontrivial normal subgroup
   contains one.

`Groups.Subgroups.Examples` evaluates the orders and decisions of these
modules on V₄, S₃ and A₅ (`Groups.Examples.Small`).  No barrel imports
it, so a module that imports this one neither checks the examples nor
takes their names.

```agda
{-# OPTIONS --without-K --exact-split --safe #-}

module Groups.Subgroups where

open import Groups.Subgroups.Basic      public
open import Groups.Subgroups.Generated  public
open import Groups.Subgroups.Group      public
open import Groups.Subgroups.Notions    public
```
